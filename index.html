Where do I go from here?

Maybe you’re considering an ADHD assessment. Maybe you already have a report and a new set of questions. A care map starts with what you’ve tried and gives you one practical next step.

Recognition & searching

“I keep losing track of tasks at work. I’d like to talk to someone, but I don’t know how to explain it.”

First step
Write down two recent examples and how each affected your day. Bring them to a conversation with your primary care provider.
A question to take with you
“Who can help me explore what might be behind these difficulties?”
Check back
After the conversation, note the next contact and who will reach out.
Founder-led pilot · Edmonton, Canada

A map you can take with you.

Justin Brokop developed ADHD Care Navigator from his experience navigating ADHD and his research during his Master of Counselling. In the planned pilot, adults in Canada meet with Justin to talk through what they’ve tried, what matters to them, and where they’re stuck.

Proposed initial session · CAD $199
Receive a written care map with a next action, questions for providers, and a point to check back. Clinical assessment and treatment stay with your healthcare providers.
